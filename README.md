## My Favorite Series: Bloodhounds

*Bloodhounds* is a South Korean action series about two young boxers who team up against a loan shark. I like how the series shows their friendship growing as they learn to trust each other outside the ring.

### Main Characters

| Character | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Kim Geon-woo | A boxer who wants to help his family |
| Hong Woo-jin | A fellow boxer who becomes his close friend |

### Why I Like It

1. The **boxing scenes** feel fast and intense.
2. The friendship between the two leads makes the story easy to get into.
3. The story gives both characters a reason to keep fighting for each other.

> My favorite part is seeing the two boxers go from rivals to friends.
